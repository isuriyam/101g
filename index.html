About 101G

A trusted technology partner for more than two decades.

101 Global Company Limited (101G) is an information technology and telecommunications company established in 1999, with registered capital of 120 million baht.

Around 98% of our business serves the government and telecommunications sectors. We deliver complete projects together with strong business partners and consortiums in Thailand and multinational companies.
